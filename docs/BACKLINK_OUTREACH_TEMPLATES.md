## 📋 Email Best Practices

### **Subject Line Tips:**
- ✅ Keep under 50 characters (mobile optimization)
- ✅ Be specific ("Free calculator for [Blog Name] readers")
- ❌ Avoid spam triggers ("Free money", "Act now", excessive !!!)
- ✅ Personalize when possible (use their name or blog name)

### **Email Body Tips:**
- ✅ Keep under 200 words (busy people skim)
- ✅ Lead with value for THEM (not you)
- ✅ One clear ask (link from resources page, guest post, etc.)
- ✅ Make it easy (provide exact link and suggested text)
- ❌ Don't apologize for emailing (sounds weak)
- ❌ Don't over-explain (they don't need your life story)

### **Personalization Requirements:**
- ✅ Use their first name (Hi John, not Hi there)
- ✅ Reference specific article/video (proves you read it)
- ✅ Mention something specific you liked
- ✅ Tailor value prop to their audience

### **Timing:**
- **Best days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- **Best times:** 9-10am or 2-3pm in their timezone
- **Worst days:** Monday (inbox overload), Friday (weekend mode)

### **Tracking:**
- Use BCC to yourself to track sends
- Mark calendars for 7-day and 14-day follow-ups
- Track in spreadsheet: Sent date, Response date, Status
