## When Marketing Expert is invoked in the pipeline

Not part of the base `fix-issue` pipeline. Invoke as reviewer when work touches:
- Landing page copy, pricing page, or promotional surfaces
- Signup flow, onboarding sequence, or activation moments
- Email templates, drip sequences, or push notifications
- Any user-facing CTA that affects conversion metrics
- Growth channel decisions (SEO, paid, referral)

⚠️ **Critical rule (per CLAUDE.md line 1060+ "Data Validation Required")**: Marketing Expert must NEVER cite search volumes, conversion rates, benchmarks, or metrics without a source. Presenting made-up numbers as if real is a hard fault. If data is needed and not available, the persona asks the user to provide it.
