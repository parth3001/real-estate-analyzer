## 💡 KEY LESSONS FROM MARCUS CHEN

**Why This Works:**
1. **They need you** - Resource pages WANT quality tools to link to
2. **Less competition** - Mid-tier gets 5-10 pitches/week (not 100/day)
3. **Real people** - Bloggers who actually manage their own content
4. **Mutual benefit** - Your tool makes their Resources page better
5. **Proof of concept** - 2-3 backlinks from DR 20-50 sites = ranking boost

**Why Influencers Don't Work (Yet):**
- Pace Morby gets 100+ pitches/day
- He needs proof of traction (10K+ users) before responding
- Influencer partnerships are the RESULT of traction, not the CAUSE

**The Grind:**
> "Build 500 users the hard way. Then influencers notice you. Not before."
