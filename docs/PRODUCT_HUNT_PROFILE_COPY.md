## Key Messaging Points That Work Well

### ✅ What to Emphasize:
1. **Builder/Maker Identity**: "Built REanalyzr" (not "Founded" or "CEO")
2. **Nights/Weekends Context**: Shows dedication without conflicting with full-time role
3. **Scratching Own Itch**: Built tool to solve own problem (authentic PH story)
4. **Mission-Driven**: Democratizing access to professional tools
5. **Real Investor**: You're a user of your own product (credibility)

### ✅ What to Avoid:
1. ❌ "Founder" or "CEO" titles (might conflict with LinkedIn)
2. ❌ Claims about leaving job or going full-time (not true yet)
3. ❌ Overly corporate language (PH community prefers authentic)
4. ❌ Revenue claims or customer numbers if not verifiable
5. ❌ Comparison attacks on competitors (PH community dislikes)

## Product Hunt Community Tips

**The PH Community Values**:
1. 🏗️ **Builders who ship**: You've built and launched (credibility)
2. 📖 **Transparent stories**: Honest about journey, not just hype
3. 🤝 **Helping others**: Your tool solves real pain points
4. 💬 **Engagement**: Respond to comments, learn from feedback
5. 🎯 **Problem-first**: Lead with problem, then solution

**Red Flags to Avoid**:
1. ❌ Overpromising features not yet built
2. ❌ Fake scarcity tactics ("only 10 spots left!")
3. ❌ Review manipulation or fake upvotes
4. ❌ Ignoring critical feedback in comments
5. ❌ Pure self-promotion without community engagement
