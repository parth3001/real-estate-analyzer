# Month 6 Decision Framework

**Purpose:** Data-driven decision on which business model to scale: B2B White-Label, B2C Subscription, or Hybrid

**Decision Point:** End of Month 6 (after 3 months of B2B validation testing)

**Last Updated:** January 29, 2026

---

## 🎯 The Strategic Question

**By Month 6, you will have tested B2B white-label for 3 months (Months 4-6).**

**The data will tell you:**
- Did educators buy? (validation of B2B model)
- Did SEO drive signups? (validation of B2C model)
- Should you scale B2B, B2C, or both?

**This framework helps you make that decision objectively, not emotionally.**

---

## 📊 Month 6 Data You'll Have

### B2B Metrics (Months 4-6 Testing)
- **Outreach:** Emails sent to validated Buy & Hold educators
- **Engagement:** Demo calls booked (% of emails)
- **Close Rate:** Customers closed (% of demo calls)
- **Revenue:** MRR from B2B customers
- **Retention:** Are Month 4 customers still paying in Month 6?

### B2C Metrics (Passive Growth)
- **Traffic:** Visitors from SEO calculators
- **Signups:** Free beta signups (% of traffic)
- **Engagement:** Active users (% of signups)
- **Testimonials:** User feedback quality
- **Revenue Potential:** Would they pay $14.99/month?

### Product Metrics (Overall Health)
- **Completion Rate:** % of users who finish full analysis
- **Return Usage:** % of users who analyze 2+ properties
- **Feature Adoption:** Which features get used most?
- **Bug Reports:** Product stability

## ✅ Scenario 1: B2B Validated → Scale B2B

### Validation Criteria

**B2B is validated if you achieve:**
- ✅ **3+ customers closed** (proof of demand)
- ✅ **Close rate: 20%+** (10 demo calls → 2+ customers)
- ✅ **MRR: $300-1,000** (3-5 customers @ $99-199/month)
- ✅ **Retention: 100%** (all customers still paying in Month 6)
- ✅ **Customer feedback: Positive** ("This is valuable for my students")

**What this tells you:**
- Educators will pay for white-label tools
- Product-market fit exists in B2B segment
- Repeatable sales process works
- Market is large enough (3 customers from 20 targets = 15% penetration)

### Recommended Action: Scale B2B

**Months 7-12 Strategy:**
- **Focus:** 80% time on B2B sales and growth
- **Target:** 20-30 B2B customers by Month 12
- **Revenue Goal:** $3,980-11,970 MRR

**Tactics:**
- Outbound to 100+ REIA chapters (email campaigns)
- Attend 2-3 regional REIA conferences (in-person demos)
- Build referral program (existing customers refer new)
- Build features customers request (multi-tenancy, educator dashboard)
- Upsell to Professional tier ($399/month)

**B2C Decision:**
- Keep product free for individual users (beta)
- SEO calculators continue compounding passively
- **Optional:** Launch $14.99/month in Month 12 (secondary revenue)

**Expected Month 12 Outcome:**
- B2B: 20-30 customers @ $199-399 avg = $3,980-11,970 MRR
- B2C: 0 MRR (still free) OR $500-2,000 MRR if launched

## ✅ Scenario 2: B2C Validated → Scale B2C

### Validation Criteria

**B2C is validated if you achieve:**
- ❌ **B2B: 0-2 customers closed** (not enough demand)
- ✅ **SEO Traffic: 200-500 visitors/month** (calculators working)
- ✅ **Signup Rate: 10-20%** (20-100 free signups/month)
- ✅ **Engagement: 30%+ return usage** (users analyze 2+ properties)
- ✅ **Testimonials: Strong positive feedback** ("This saved me hours")
- ✅ **Willingness to Pay: Survey shows 20%+ would pay $14.99/month**

**What this tells you:**
- Individual investors love the product
- SEO is working (passive traffic growing)
- Product-market fit exists in B2C segment
- Educators don't see enough value (or wrong target selection)

### Recommended Action: Scale B2C

**Months 7-12 Strategy:**
- **Focus:** 80% time on B2C growth (SEO, content, conversion)
- **Target:** 600-800 paid B2C users by Month 12
- **Revenue Goal:** $8,994-11,992 MRR

**Tactics:**
- Launch $14.99/month subscription (Month 7)
- Write 10 long-form SEO guides (hire content writer with early revenue)
- Optimize conversion funnel (free → paid)
- YouTube content doubles down (2-3 videos/week)
- Paid ads test ($250-500/month if organic needs boost)

**B2B Decision:**
- Stop active B2B outreach (don't waste time)
- Accept inbound B2B inquiries only (if they come to you)
- Focus 100% on individual investor market

**Expected Month 12 Outcome:**
- B2C: 600-800 paid users @ $14.99 = $8,994-11,992 MRR
- B2B: 0-2 opportunistic customers = $0-400 MRR

## ⚖️ Scenario 3: Hybrid Validated → Scale Both

### Validation Criteria

**Hybrid is validated if you achieve:**
- ⚠️ **B2B: 2-3 customers closed** (some demand, not overwhelming)
- ⚠️ **Close Rate: 15-25%** (decent but not amazing)
- ✅ **SEO Traffic: 200-500 visitors/month** (passive growth happening)
- ✅ **Signup Rate: 10-20%** (B2C interest exists)
- ✅ **Both segments show promise** (neither is a clear winner)

**What this tells you:**
- Both B2B and B2C can work
- Neither is dominant enough to go all-in
- Diversification reduces risk
- You can serve both markets

### Recommended Action: Hybrid Model

**Months 7-12 Strategy:**
- **Focus:** 60% time B2B, 40% time B2C
- **Target:** 15-20 B2B customers + 400-600 B2C users
- **Revenue Goal:** $8,000-12,000 MRR combined

**Tactics:**

**B2B (60% time):**
- Outbound to 50 more REIA chapters (vs 100+ in pure B2B)
- Attend 1-2 conferences (vs 2-3 in pure B2B)
- Build multi-tenancy and educator dashboard
- Target: 15-20 B2B customers by Month 12

**B2C (40% time):**
- Launch $14.99/month subscription (Month 7)
- Write 5 long-form SEO guides (vs 10 in pure B2C)
- Optimize conversion funnel
- Target: 400-600 paid users by Month 12

**Expected Month 12 Outcome:**
- B2B: 15-20 customers @ $299 avg = $4,485-5,980 MRR
- B2C: 400-600 users @ $14.99 = $5,996-8,994 MRR
- **Total: $10,481-14,974 MRR**

**Why Hybrid Makes Sense:**
- Diversified revenue (less risky than single channel)
- B2B provides high-value relationships
- B2C provides passive, scalable income
- Both markets benefit from product improvements

## ❌ Scenario 4: Neither Validated → Pivot or Iterate

### Red Flag Criteria

**Neither model is validated if:**
- ❌ **B2B: 0-1 customers** (no demand after 20+ outreaches)
- ❌ **Close Rate: <10%** (product-market fit questionable)
- ❌ **SEO Traffic: <100 visitors/month** (calculators not ranking)
- ❌ **Signup Rate: <5%** (poor conversion)
- ❌ **Engagement: <20% return usage** (users don't find value)

**What this tells you:**
- Product-market fit not found
- Target customer assumptions were wrong
- OR: Execution issues (product bugs, poor messaging)
- OR: Market timing wrong

### Recommended Action: Diagnose → Pivot or Iterate

**Step 1: Diagnose the Problem (Week 1-2)**

**B2B Diagnosis:**
- **Question:** Why didn't educators buy?
  - Was pricing too high? ($99/month → test $49/month)
  - Wrong target customers? (Dr. Ryan Chaw mistake → re-validate)
  - Product not valuable? (missing features educators need)
  - Poor sales execution? (pitch unclear, demos ineffective)

**B2C Diagnosis:**
- **Question:** Why didn't SEO work?
  - Calculators not ranking? (check Google Search Console)
  - Low signup conversion? (UX issues, unclear value prop)
  - Users don't return? (product quality issues)

**Step 2: Choose Pivot or Iterate (Week 3-4)**

**Option A: Iterate (If Close to Product-Market Fit)**
- Fix identified issues (pricing, target customers, features)
- Test for another 3 months (Months 7-9)
- Re-evaluate at Month 9

**Option B: Pivot (If Fundamentally Wrong)**
- Different target customer (e.g., real estate agents instead of investors)
- Different product (e.g., CRM for investors instead of analysis tools)
- Different business model (e.g., one-time purchase instead of subscription)

**Option C: Shut Down (If No Path Forward)**
- Admit defeat, learn lessons
- Move on to next idea
- Don't throw good money after bad

**This scenario is unlikely if you follow the validation framework correctly, but it's important to have a plan.**

## 🔑 Key Principles

1. **Let data decide, not hope**
   - Emotions lie, metrics don't
   - 3+ customers = validated demand
   - <3 customers = not validated (yet)

2. **Both can work, but focus matters**
   - Hybrid is viable but slower growth
   - Pure plays (B2B or B2C) scale faster
   - Choose based on your strengths

3. **Pivot quickly if neither works**
   - Don't waste 12 months on wrong path
   - Month 6 decision is critical
   - Sunk cost fallacy is dangerous

4. **Build what customers ask for**
   - B2B customers say "I need X" → build X
   - B2C users say "I wish it had Y" → build Y
   - Don't speculate features

5. **Your time is the constraint**
   - 20-25 hours/week available
   - Pure play (B2B or B2C) is more efficient
   - Hybrid works but requires discipline
