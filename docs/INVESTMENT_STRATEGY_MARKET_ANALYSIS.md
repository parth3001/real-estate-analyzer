## Investment Strategy Descriptions

### 1. Buy & Hold (Traditional Long-Term Rental)

**Strategy Overview**:
- Purchase property, rent to tenant, hold for 10-30 years
- Cash flow from rent minus expenses
- Wealth building through appreciation + mortgage paydown

**Investor Profile**:
- **Age**: 28-45 years old
- **Background**: W-2 professionals with stable income
- **Risk Tolerance**: Low to medium
- **Goal**: Passive income, retirement planning

**Key Metrics**:
- Monthly cash flow
- Cap rate (5-8% typical)
- Cash-on-Cash return (6-10% typical)
- IRR (8-12% typical over 10 years)

**Tool Requirements**:
- Standard mortgage calculations
- Rental income vs operating expenses
- Long-term appreciation projections
- Tax benefits (depreciation)

---

### 2. BRRRR (Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat)

**Strategy Overview**:
- Buy distressed property below market value
- Rehab to increase value (force appreciation)
- Rent to tenant (stabilize property for refinance)
- Refinance at higher ARV (After Repair Value) to pull capital out
- Repeat process with recovered capital

**Investor Profile**:
- **Age**: 30-50 years old
- **Background**: Construction, contracting, or aggressive scaling investors
- **Risk Tolerance**: Medium to high
- **Goal**: Rapid portfolio growth, "infinite return" (zero money left in deal)

**Key Metrics**:
- **Total Investment**: Purchase + Rehab + Closing Costs
- **ARV (After Repair Value)**: Property value post-renovation
- **Refinance Amount**: 70-75% of ARV (typical)
- **Capital Recovered**: Refinance proceeds - original purchase
- **Capital Remaining in Deal**: Total investment - capital recovered
- **Effective Cash-on-Cash**: Annual cash flow ÷ capital remaining
- **Infinite Return**: When capital remaining ≤ 0 (all money pulled out)

**Tool Requirements**:
- Rehab budget estimation
- ARV calculation (comparable sales analysis)
- Refinance scenario modeling (70-75% LTV of ARV)
- Seasoning period consideration (6-12 months before refinance)
- Capital recovered vs remaining calculations
- **CRITICAL**: IRR is NOT relevant (investors hold forever, no exit date)

**Why Josh Lupo Said "IRR Doesn't Matter"**:
- BRRRR investors hold properties indefinitely (infinite hold period)
- IRR calculation requires assumed sale date
- When you achieve "infinite return" (all capital recovered), IRR formula breaks
- Novices care about monthly cash flow, not 10-year return projections

---

### 3. House Hacking

**Strategy Overview**:
- Buy 2-4 unit property (duplex, triplex, fourplex)
- Live in one unit, rent out others
- FHA loan allows 3.5% down payment (owner-occupied)
- Reduce or eliminate housing expense while building equity

**Investor Profile**:
- **Age**: 25-35 years old
- **Background**: First-time real estate investors
- **Risk Tolerance**: Low (living in the property reduces risk perception)
- **Goal**: Eliminate housing expense, first investment property

**Key Metrics**:
- **Owner-Occupancy Cost**: Mortgage - rental income from other units
- **Effective Housing Cost Reduction**: Pre-rent expense - post-rent expense
- Monthly cash flow (often negative, but offset by free/cheap housing)
- Equity building (mortgage paydown + appreciation)

**Tool Requirements**:
- Multi-unit property support (2-4 units)
- Owner-occupied unit designation (not generating income)
- FHA loan calculations (3.5% down, MIP)
- Housing cost comparison (rent vs own with rental income)

---

### 4. Fix & Flip

**Strategy Overview**:
- Buy distressed property, renovate, sell quickly (6-12 months)
- Profit from forced appreciation (not market appreciation)
- Active income strategy (not passive cash flow)

**Investor Profile**:
- **Age**: 30-55 years old
- **Background**: Full-time investors, construction professionals
- **Risk Tolerance**: High (market timing risk, renovation risk)
- **Goal**: Active income, rapid capital deployment

**Key Metrics**:
- **Purchase Price**
- **Rehab Budget**
- **Holding Costs**: Mortgage, utilities, taxes during renovation
- **ARV (After Repair Value)**
- **Sale Proceeds**: ARV - selling costs (6-8%)
- **Net Profit**: Sale proceeds - (purchase + rehab + holding + selling costs)
- **ROI**: Net profit ÷ total investment
- **Annualized ROI**: ROI adjusted for hold period

**Tool Requirements**:
- Rehab budget tracking
- Holding cost calculations (time-based)
- Selling cost estimation (6% realtor, 2% closing)
- Short-term capital gains tax (37% vs 20% long-term)

**NOTE**: Not a priority for initial implementation (different business model - active vs passive income)

---

### 5. Short-Term Rental (Airbnb/VRBO)

**Strategy Overview**:
- Buy property in tourist/business travel market
- Rent nightly/weekly on platforms (Airbnb, VRBO)
- Higher cash flow than long-term rental (2-3x typical)
- More management intensive

**Investor Profile**:
- **Age**: 28-45 years old
- **Background**: Tech-savvy, hospitality, service-oriented
- **Risk Tolerance**: Medium (regulatory risk, vacancy risk)
- **Goal**: Maximum cash flow, lifestyle flexibility

**Key Metrics**:
- **Average Daily Rate (ADR)**
- **Occupancy Rate** (seasonal variation)
- **Gross Rental Income**: ADR × occupied nights
- **Operating Expenses**: Higher than long-term (utilities, cleaning, supplies)
- **Management Costs**: 20-30% (vs 8-10% for long-term)
- **Net Cash Flow**: Gross income - expenses - management

**Tool Requirements**:
- Seasonal occupancy modeling
- Higher operating expense defaults (utilities, cleaning, supplies)
- Dynamic pricing analysis
- Regulatory risk assessment (cities banning STR)

**NOTE**: Growing market but regulatory headwinds make it lower priority
