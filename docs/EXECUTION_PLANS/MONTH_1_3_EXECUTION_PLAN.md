**Timeline:** Weeks 1-12
**Goal:** Build passive SEO assets + validate target customers
**Time Commitment:** 20-25 hours/week

## 🎯 Phase Overview

**What You're Building:**
- 3 SEO calculators (passive traffic engines)

## 🚫 What NOT to Do (Avoid Distractions)

### Don't Build These Yet
- ❌ Multi-tenancy (no customers asking)
- ❌ Educator dashboard (no customers paying)
- ❌ Feature flags (no Professional tier customers)
- ❌ API access (Enterprise tier, way too early)
- ❌ Mobile app (not on roadmap)

### Don't Spend Time On
- ❌ Complex blog posts (SEO calculators are enough)
- ❌ Social media beyond YouTube (focus)
- ❌ Paid ads (too early, no validation yet)
- ❌ Perfect product (ship, learn, iterate)
- ❌ Networking events (focus on research)

### Don't Overthink
- ❌ Pricing tiers (decide Month 6 based on data)
- ❌ B2B vs B2C (decide Month 6 based on validation)
- ❌ Feature roadmap (let customers tell you)
