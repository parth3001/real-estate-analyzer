**Analyzr** is a real estate investment analysis platform built on a simple principle: *Individual investors should have access to the same tools and methodologies that institutional investors use—without needing a finance degree to operate them.*

Stop copying and pasting from 6 different tabs. Our 4-step wizard:

- **Address Lookup**: Enter an address → We auto-populate property details, tax assessments, and rent estimates from public records and MLS data (85-95% accuracy)
- **Smart Defaults**: Market-based assumptions for vacancy rates, maintenance, and appreciation specific to your metro area
- **Validation**: Real-time warnings if numbers look off (e.g., "Property taxes seem 40% higher than area average")

**Time saved**: 45-60 minutes of manual data entry per property.

#### **2. Investment Decision Engine (Professional Verdict in Seconds)**

Our proprietary scoring algorithm analyzes 50+ data points to give you a clear verdict:

- 🟢 **BUY (75-100/100)**: Strong fundamentals, meets professional standards
- 🔵 **NEGOTIATE (60-74/100)**: Decent property, but overpriced—here's your walk-away price
- 🟡 **CAUTION (45-59/100)**: Marginal deal with significant concerns
- 🔴 **PASS (0-44/100)**: Does not meet minimum investment criteria

**The scoring breakdown:**
- Cash Flow Quality (30 points)
- Cap Rate Performance (20 points)
- IRR Strength (20 points)
- Cash-on-Cash Return (15 points)
- DSCR Safety (15 points)

**Why it matters**: Our engine is intentionally conservative. A "NEGOTIATE" verdict doesn't mean it's a bad property—it means you should negotiate for a better price. We've validated our calculations against institutional underwriting standards from Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, and Wall Street Prep.

**Accuracy**: 75-100% verdict accuracy across 50+ realistic test scenarios.

We're one of the only platforms that handles **both single-family AND multi-family** properties with the same level of sophistication:

- **Unit Mix Analysis**: Track performance by unit type (Studios, 1BR, 2BR, 3BR)
- **Institutional Metrics**: DSCR, Debt Yield, Break-Even Occupancy, GRM—the same metrics lenders use
- **NOI Calculations**: Following Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac standards (yes, we know vacancy reduces income, not expenses)
- **Commercial Financing**: Model balloon payments and 5/1, 7/1, 10/1 ARM structures

**Market gap**: Most "rental calculators" stop at 4 units. We go up to 32-unit complexes with full institutional-grade analysis.

#### **4. AI-Enhanced Market Intelligence**

We integrate GPT-4o for context-aware insights:
