# Target Customer Validation Guide

**Purpose:** Avoid the "Dr. Ryan Chaw mistake" - validate educators teach what you can analyze BEFORE outreach

**Last Updated:** January 29, 2026

---

## 🎯 The Dr. Ryan Chaw Lesson

**What Happened:**
- Identified Dr. Ryan Chaw as potential target educator
- Started research for outreach
- Discovered: He teaches student housing conversion (not Buy & Hold rentals)
- **Conclusion:** Wrong product fit, would have wasted outreach effort

**The Lesson:**
**ALWAYS validate product fit BEFORE adding to target list**

---

## ✅ Ideal Target Customer Profile

### Buy & Hold Rental Property Educators

**What they teach:**
- ✅ Traditional rental property investing
- ✅ Cash flow analysis (monthly rent - expenses)
- ✅ Long-term wealth building through rentals
- ✅ 1-4 unit residential properties
- ✅ Beginner to intermediate investors

**Examples of topics:**
- "How to analyze rental properties"
- "Buy and hold real estate investing"
- "Rental property cash flow analysis"
- "Building wealth with rental properties"
- "First rental property for beginners"

**Business model:**
- Paid courses ($500-$5,000 per student)
- Coaching programs (1-on-1 or group)
- 2-4 cohorts per year
- 50-500 students per cohort
- Estimated revenue: $100K-$500K annually

**Current tools:**
- Excel spreadsheets (manual analysis)
- BiggerPockets calculator (free, not white-labeled)
- Google Sheets templates
- No proprietary analysis tools

**Size:**
- 1,000-50,000 followers/subscribers
- Active course or coaching program
- Not mega-influencers (too big to reach)
- Not hobbyists (can't afford $199-399/month)

## ❌ Wrong Target Customers (AVOID)

### Student Housing Conversion Educators
**Why wrong:** You don't analyze conversion costs, zoning, per-bedroom rent modeling
**Example:** Dr. Ryan Chaw

### Commercial Real Estate Educators
**Why wrong:** You don't analyze triple-net leases, CAM expenses, commercial cap rates
**Example:** Retail, office, industrial property courses

### Large Multi-Family Syndication (50+ Units)
**Why wrong:** You don't have syndication waterfall, LP/GP splits, SEC compliance tools
**Example:** Apartment syndication courses

### Fix-and-Flip Educators
**Why wrong:** You don't have ARV calculators, rehab cost estimators, flip timeline tools
**Example:** House flipping courses (unless they also teach rentals)

### Short-Term Rental (Airbnb) Educators
**Why wrong:** You don't analyze nightly rates, occupancy modeling, STR-specific expenses
**Example:** Airbnb arbitrage courses

### Motivational/Mindset Only
**Why wrong:** They don't teach mechanics or analysis
**Example:** "Get rich with real estate" mindset coaches

## 🔍 Validation Checklist (Use for Each Educator)

### Step 1: Product Fit Validation

**Research their content (YouTube, blog, course page):**

**Question 1: What property types do they teach?**
- [ ] Single-family rentals (1-4 units) → ✅ GOOD FIT
- [ ] BRRRR strategy → ✅ GOOD FIT (after bug fixes)
- [ ] Small multi-family (2-4 units) → ✅ GOOD FIT (after bug fixes)
- [ ] Student housing conversion → ❌ BAD FIT
- [ ] Commercial (retail, office, industrial) → ❌ BAD FIT
- [ ] Large syndication (50+ units) → ❌ BAD FIT
- [ ] Fix-and-flip only → ❌ BAD FIT
- [ ] Short-term rentals (Airbnb) → ❌ BAD FIT

**Question 2: Do they teach analysis mechanics?**
- [ ] Shows how to calculate cash flow → ✅ GOOD FIT
- [ ] Explains cap rate, cash-on-cash return → ✅ GOOD FIT
- [ ] Uses Excel or BiggerPockets in teaching → ✅ PERFECT FIT
- [ ] Only teaches strategy/mindset (no numbers) → ❌ BAD FIT

**Question 3: What tools do they currently use?**
- [ ] Excel spreadsheets → ✅ PERFECT (they need your tool)
- [ ] BiggerPockets calculator → ✅ GOOD (you're better + white-label)
- [ ] No analysis tools → ✅ GOOD (gap in their offering)
- [ ] Custom-built calculator → ⚠️ MAYBE (high switching cost)
- [ ] Partnership with another software → ❌ BAD FIT (contractual conflict)

---

### Step 2: Business Model Validation

**Question 4: Do they run paid courses/cohorts?**
- [ ] Paid courses ($500-$5,000 per student) → ✅ GOOD (can afford $199-399/month)
- [ ] Coaching programs → ✅ GOOD
- [ ] Free content only → ❌ BAD FIT (no budget)

**Question 5: How often do they run cohorts?**
- [ ] 2-4 cohorts per year → ✅ PERFECT (recurring need)
- [ ] One-off course (no recurring cohorts) → ⚠️ MAYBE (lower ongoing value)
- [ ] Continuous enrollment → ✅ GOOD (always need tools)

**Question 6: How many students?**
- [ ] 50-200 students per cohort → ✅ PERFECT (unlimited users = huge value)
- [ ] 10-50 students → ✅ GOOD
- [ ] 200+ students → ✅ EXCELLENT
- [ ] <10 students → ⚠️ MAYBE (early stage, may not afford tools)

**Question 7: Estimated revenue?**
- [ ] $100K-$500K annual revenue → ✅ PERFECT TARGET
- [ ] $50K-$100K → ✅ GOOD (can afford $199/month)
- [ ] $500K-$1M → ✅ EXCELLENT (can afford $399-799/month)
- [ ] <$50K → ❌ TOO SMALL (can't afford subscription)
- [ ] $1M+ → ⚠️ MAYBE (may build in-house or partner with bigger platform)

---

### Step 3: Reachability Validation

**Question 8: Can you reach them?**
- [ ] Active on social media (responds to comments) → ✅ GOOD
- [ ] Email address visible (contact page) → ✅ GOOD
- [ ] LinkedIn active → ✅ GOOD
- [ ] No contact info, never responds → ❌ BAD FIT

**Question 9: Size/influence level?**
- [ ] 1K-10K followers → ✅ PERFECT (reachable, responsive)
- [ ] 10K-50K followers → ✅ GOOD (still reachable)
- [ ] 50K-100K followers → ⚠️ MAYBE (harder to reach, worth trying)
- [ ] 100K-1M followers → ❌ TOO BIG (won't respond to cold outreach)

## 🎯 Target List Structure

### Tier 1: Top Priority (5 educators)
**Criteria:**
- Perfect product fit (teaches exactly what you analyze)
- Uses Excel today (clear pain point)
- 50-200 students per cohort (unlimited users = huge value)
- Active, responsive (likely to engage)
- $100K-$500K revenue (can afford $199-399/month)

**Outreach timing:** Month 4 (first wave)

---

### Tier 2: Good Fit (10 educators)
**Criteria:**
- Good product fit (teaches mostly what you analyze)
- Uses BiggerPockets or no tools (room for improvement)
- 20-100 students per cohort
- Moderately active
- $50K-$300K revenue

**Outreach timing:** Month 4-5 (second wave)

---

### Tier 3: Stretch Targets (5 educators)
**Criteria:**
- Partial product fit (teach some of what you analyze)
- Larger following (50K-100K) but harder to reach
- OR: Smaller revenue (<$100K) but high growth potential
- OR: Uses custom tools (high switching cost) but worth trying

**Outreach timing:** Month 6+ (after validation with Tier 1-2)

## 🔍 Research Methods

### Method 1: YouTube Search
```
Search queries:
- "rental property analysis tutorial"
- "how to analyze rental properties"
- "buy and hold real estate investing"
- "rental property cash flow analysis"
- "BRRRR strategy tutorial"

Filters:
- 10K-100K subscribers (sweet spot)
- Active uploads (within last 3 months)

What to look for:
- Do they show Excel in videos? (PERFECT)
- Do they teach step-by-step analysis? (GOOD)
- Course link in description? (BUSINESS MODEL)
```

---

### Method 2: Google Search
```
Search queries:
- "rental property investing course"
- "real estate investing coaching program"
- "buy and hold rental course"

Filters:
- Active courses (not just blogs)
- Clear pricing (indicates serious business)
- Student testimonials (proof of cohorts)

What to look for:
- Course curriculum (do they teach analysis?)
- Tools mentioned (Excel, BP, custom?)
- Pricing ($500-$5,000 = can afford subscription)
```

---

### Method 3: BiggerPockets Forums
```
Where to look:
- Active forum contributors
- Forum signatures with course links
- "Diary" threads (investors documenting journey)

What to look for:
- Teaching others (not just learning)
- Mentions of courses/coaching
- Uses BP calculator in posts (we're better + white-label)
```

### Method 4: Teachable/Kajabi Course Marketplaces
```
Browse:
- Real estate category

- "Deal analysis" or "Property evaluation" in curriculum

```

## ⚠️ Red Flags (Skip These)

### Content Red Flags
- ❌ Talks about "commercial deals" or "syndication"
- ❌ Focuses on "student housing" or "conversion projects"
- ❌ Only teaches motivation/mindset (no analysis mechanics)
- ❌ Exclusively fix-and-flip content (no rental focus)
- ❌ Short-term rental (Airbnb) only

### Business Red Flags
- ❌ Free content only (no paid courses = no budget)
- ❌ Affiliate marketer only (promoting others' courses)
- ❌ Inactive (no recent content in 6+ months)
- ❌ Already partnered with a software tool (contractual conflict)

### Reachability Red Flags
- ❌ No contact information anywhere
- ❌ Never responds to comments/emails (tested by community)
- ❌ Mega-influencer (1M+ followers, won't see your outreach)

### Content Green Flags
- ✅ Shows Excel spreadsheets in videos
- ✅ "How to analyze rental properties" content
- ✅ Uses BiggerPockets calculator (we're better)
- ✅ Teaches cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash return
- ✅ BRRRR or house hacking focus (we analyze this)

### Business Green Flags
- ✅ Clear course pricing ($1,000-$5,000 per student)
- ✅ Runs cohorts 2-4x per year (recurring need)
- ✅ 50-200 students per cohort (unlimited users = huge value)
- ✅ Student testimonials (proof of active business)
- ✅ No existing calculator/tool (gap we fill)

### Reachability Green Flags
- ✅ Active on social media (responds to comments)
- ✅ Email on website contact page
- ✅ LinkedIn profile active
- ✅ 5K-50K followers (reachable, not too big)
