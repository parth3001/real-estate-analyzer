## 🚫 **IMPORTANT: What NOT to Do**

### **Don't Ask Strangers for Upvotes**
- ❌ Don't DM random PH users asking for upvotes (considered spam)
- ❌ Don't post in Facebook groups "upvote my product" (against PH rules)
- ❌ Don't use upvote exchange services (PH detects and penalizes)

### **Don't Over-Explain Product Hunt**
- ✅ Keep explanation simple: "It's like app store homepage for new products"
- ❌ Don't send 5-page explanation (people won't read it)

### **Don't Be Too Demanding**
- ✅ "No pressure, even just an upvote helps!"
- ❌ "You MUST be online at 12:01am or this won't work!"

### **Don't Spam Follow-Ups**
- ✅ Send 1 initial email + 1 reminder
- ❌ Don't email 5 times if they don't respond (respect their time)
